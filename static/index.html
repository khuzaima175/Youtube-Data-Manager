Onboarding Tour
⚡ Velocity (/day)
Views accrued per day ($views / age$). Measures current virality velocity rather than lifetime cumulative total.
🔥 hotScore
Formula $recentAvg \times \log_2(n+1)$ measuring niche interest and current viewer appetite for a specific topic.
📈 Topic Momentum
Ratio of recent average views to older historical views for a topic ($>1.2\times$ = surging trend).
⚔️ Threat Overlap (Jaccard Index)
Mathematical topic overlap percentage ($|A \cap B| / |A \cup B|$) comparing your top-20 topics against a rival's catalog.
🌲 Evergreen Fingerprint
Percentage of a channel's top-10 videos that are $>1\text{ year old}$. Distinguishes long-tail compounders from hype chasers.
⚡ Collision Shadow
Flags your underperforming drops that were published within $\pm 24\text{h}$ of a competitor $\ge 1.8\times$ your size on a shared topic.
▲/▼ Health Multipliers
Ratio of a video's view performance against your long-form 30-video average ($▲ \ge 1.3\times$ = Overperformer, $▼ \le 0.7\times$ = Underperformer).
⏰ Timing Intelligence (Best-Time Heatmap)
Slot velocity = mean views/day of videos aged 3–180d in that 2-hour day/time bucket. Hatched cells = fewer than 3 videos (low confidence).
